Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

This OAuth application is a personal backup utility. It is used by a single person, its owner, to copy their own files between their own computer and their own Google Drive account.

  • The application accesses Google Drive only on behalf of the signed-in owner.
  • No data obtained through Google APIs is collected, stored on any server, shared with, sold or transferred to any third party.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
